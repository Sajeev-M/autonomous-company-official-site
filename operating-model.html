Company/Operating model

Capability needs
a clear operating model.

AI-assisted work should make responsibility easier to follow. Our model connects specialist work with independent review and human oversight.

Human direction

The owner defines goals, authorizes consequential actions and decides whether reviewed work should move forward.

Coordination

A CEO-agent role and departmental coordinators translate goals into bounded responsibilities. A role name is not proof of active execution.

Specialist work

Engineering, research, sales, marketing and operations contribute according to the agreed scope and available tools.

Independent examination

Security and quality roles inspect the work. A generated response alone is not evidence of delivery or a safe release.

From a need to reviewed work.

  1. Discover

    Research the problem, evidence and constraints.

  2. Architect

    Agree scope, responsibilities and acceptance criteria.

  3. Build

    Develop a reviewable artifact or bounded prototype.

  4. Verify

    Inspect artifacts and critical paths independently.

  5. Improve

    Propose changes from observed results and feedback.
